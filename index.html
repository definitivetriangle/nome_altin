Background

Nome was born and raised in the Altin Family, a mafia gang that has operated for generations, back to the ’70s or even the ’60s. Her father is the current head of the family. Nome is expected to take his seat when his time runs out.

She was raised differently from other kids. Economics, sociology, politics, history, geography and advanced calculus were drilled into her from a young age, under a very strict system.

In her teens she developed a taste for pulling strings from behind the stage. It began with harmless pranks and slowly turned into butterfly-effect schemes that ran for months. Around then her father brought home a lost boy named Rel. They grew up as childhood friends.

Rel’s last commission was to capture the anomaly known as “Daisy, the oddity,” who is in a different world called Cier. Nome insisted he invite her along to explore it. Maybe even to leave her mark there.

Abilities

Blastema cells

Simple Regeneration.

She regenerates cuts, lost limbs and damaged organs. Seconds for a scratch, hours or days for the worst. A severed forearm takes about an hour.

It heals her fast, but not instantly and not endlessly. Her body can tank hits on the scale of an average building, yet a plain gun can still do the job if it deals more damage than she can heal.

Enhanced senses

Wolf hearing, smell and reflexes.

They let her dodge danger, sense traps and notice anything wrong with the space around her. Her reactions reach roughly mach 8–9, though acting on them is another matter.

It cuts both ways, and it can be used against her. Fireworks unnerve her, gunshots can briefly stun her, strong odors sicken her, and hallucinogenic effects hit her much harder.

Underwater breathing

Axolotl gills.

She breathes underwater like a normal axolotl. She is still only a mediocre swimmer.

Staying submerged isn’t a problem for her, but breathing underwater and fighting underwater are different things. The gills keep her alive without making the water her territory.

Astaroth

The Altin Family is rumored to hold a contract with an eldritch being. If true, it gives them influence over the country greater than the government’s, and blood relatives are tuned to it, making witchcraft easy. The price: later generations never pass 38, and usually die in their twenties.

Nome has never directly confirmed or denied it. Dismissing this as a silly fairytale.

Astaroth’s Sigil

A special sigil that gives access to her other abilities.

Nome can utilize something called “Astaroth’s Sigil”, a sigil closely related to the eldritch by the same name. At its center is a small round circle divided into four quadrants by a cross. A single straight stroke runs through the circle from upper left to lower right. It extends well past the circle on both ends.

This sigil is required for “Command” or “Hijack” to work. Placing the sigil on top of a target is as easy as using a permanent marker and scribbling it on. Though any means of etching the sigil on the target works.

To activate the sigil, she needs to use an incantation. The sigil will faintly pulse red if done correctly. The specific phrase used in the incantation is not known to many, and Nome makes sure it stays that way. The target does not feel the activation of the sigil.

Only after the sigil has been activated can any of the relating abilities below be utilized. If the sigil has been removed, Nome has to place a new one and recite the incantation all over again.

The sigil will not fade away over time. It will stay there indefinitely until someone does something about it. The sigil is resistant to any attempts of erasure, though not too hard.

Fear can improve the effectiveness of the sigil, and by extension her abilities by a long shot.

Command

Nome can command people to do anything after placing her sigil.

The most straight forward ability. Nome can overwrite the target's will to impose her own through verbal commands. Once Nome gives a command, the target will execute it as best as they can. This can work between distant phone calls and general audio recordings. As long as it’s Nome’s voice.

The length in which the target is under Nome’s influence is fully dependent on their will. As long as they’re under the sigil’s influence, they can perform multiple tasks. Targets cannot remember what happened while they were under the influence.

After the target has broken through the influence, they gain a small immunity from the sigil’s influence. Meaning that for a short while, they are immune to Command or Hijack.

Trying to command huge groups of targets is difficult because Nome’s command can scatter between the many targets. Making them less effective, inaccurate, or straight up not working. She’ll have to put in a lot of effort if she wants to command a huge group.

Hijack

Nome can take over people’s bodies and borrow their senses.

A bit more complicated than Command. Hijack takes control of the target's body and hands them over to Nome where she can utilize them as much as she wants. Naturally, her own body will fall limp and vulnerable.

The length of possession depends on the willpower of the target. The target doesn’t remember anything while they’re being hijacked.

In addition, Nome can “borrow” senses with this ability. For example; seeing, hearing, or feeling through a target. The target does not get affected in any way while Nome borrows their senses.

There’s a small period of dizziness when Nome exits Hijack, in which she cannot utilize her other sigil abilities. This dizziness can stack up if Nome uses multiple Hijacks per day. Exceeding more than six a day can seriously fry her brain. Borrowing senses does not have these drawbacks.

Weapons

Pistol: “The Dealmaker”

Light, powerful, based on the SIG P385.

Kept mostly hidden in a gun pouch behind her waist. It fires custom hollow-point .45 ACP made by Rel.

She dislikes using it, and saves it for long range, flying targets and dire situations.

Handguns only fill the gaps in her close-range style. She also has to account for her own senses: gunshots can briefly stun her.

Survival knife

A standard survival knife, pretty sharp.

Tucked behind her waist, and used for combat and everything else. Based on the Böker Magnum M-Spec.

Alongside her Taekwondo and kickboxing, it fills the gaps in her close-range fighting, and unlike the pistol it works for everyday jobs too.

Strengths & weaknesses

  • ManipulationOne of her strongest aspects is definitely her ability to manipulate others in an almost uncanny way. She’s able to deduce people’s state of emotion with a glance by close observation, pattern recognition, and raw deduction. Nome knows just the right buttons to push in someone to influence them. No matter who they are.
  • CombatNome is experienced in combat, especially in close Hand-to-Hand. Partly due to the place she grew up in and the teaching from professionals. Her preferred fighting style is Taekwondo and Kick-Boxing. That’s due to her strong legs which she takes pride in. However, she does not shy from using one-handed weapons such as knives, daggers, and handheld guns to add to her fighting style.
  • Biological prowessBeing a Wolf Hybrid gives her an edge in reaction time, agility, and overall speed. Which she uses to her full extent to often set up and execute plans during battle. Her reaction time in particular is pretty high. She can react to around mach 8-9, acting on it however, is a different story.
  • IntellectIt may not seem like it at first, but Nome is pretty intelligent. Especially in battle. Her ability to act according to the situation and adaptive thinking is what kept her going through all her scuffles. In other scenarios, Nome is pretty resourceful and has great foresight with her intuition which allows her to form sophisticated plans and strategies on the spot.
  • HomeworkNome is not foolish enough to enter a fight without knowledge of the opponent. So whenever she gets the chance. She’ll have researched a thing or two about them. Naturally, sudden ambushes make that impossible.
  • Physical limitsEven with her biological advantages, she is as durable as one may think. Something simple like a gun could prove to be a reliable way to deal with her if used correctly. As long as they can inflict more damage than she can heal from.
  • Double-edged sensesHer enhanced senses can act as an advantage and a disturbance at the same time. Sounds like fireworks can really unnerve Nome, sudden gunshots can stun her slightly, and strong odors can make her sick. Hallucinative abilities that mess with senses are way more effective on her.
  • Spoiled bratBeing the daughter of the mafia boss developed some pride and ego in her. That ego is decently fragile and can be exploited to ragebait her.
  • Delicate sigilsThe sigils she uses to mark targets are actually simple enough to remove. If the surface where the sigil was placed on is removed from the main body, then it wouldn't work anymore. For example; If the sigil was placed on a shirt, removing the shirt would nullify the sigils ability until the shirt has been put on again.

Personality

On the surface Nome seems like a silly goofball. But inside, she’s most likely scheming in some way behind the scenes. People who know who she really is know better than to trust her word, even if she does everything in her power to appear otherwise. Motive, reactions, relationships, and more can easily be fabricated to the point where the line of truths and lies are too blurred to be distinguished.

All to say that she keeps an act towards most people. The only exceptions are people she likes and trusts. But even then, Nome shows only what they need to know, nothing less, nothing more.

Nome learned to be cunning, charming, and manipulative after growing up in the Mafia underworld. Her ability to assess people’s emotions with a glance or two is something she picked up after observing, studying, and testing countless people’s behaviours for most of her life. She knows the ins-and-outs of the mind like the back of her hand.

A sort of rule formed between the people she had interacted with a lot, and it’s a simple rule: “You can’t hide from Nome.”

Questions

Does the sigil need anything to work?

Mostly no. She places it on the target and it works. A target who fears Nome makes it work better.

Can she jumpscare someone and activate it instantly?

No. A jumpscare is only surprise. It would be funny if she tried.

Can people resist it?

Yes and no. The best they can do is shorten the time they’re influenced, to about six seconds at minimum. Strong-willed people with strong souls have the potential to resist.

Can outside forces pull someone out?

Very much so. The easiest way is removing the sigil. If it’s on clothing, take it off. If it’s on skin, cut that part off.

Can a mage or witch use the sigil?

Yes, but since they aren’t closely tied to Astaroth, the results are usually weaker.

How does she usually apply it?

Permanent marker is the easiest. It only has to be readable. Carving it into skin also works.

Does it work more than once?

If someone breaks out, they stay open to her commands and hijacks until the sigil is taken off their body.

What if her body is hurt while she hijacks?

She won’t feel it. The pain arrives all at once when she returns to her body.

Can she use a hijacked body’s wings or other foreign limbs?

No. She’d have to learn how to use new limbs first.

Does she keep the hijacked person’s memories?

Somewhat. She can reach about the previous 45 to 60 minutes. Beyond that it’s too foggy.
